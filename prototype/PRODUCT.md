# Customelo storefront prototype

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Standalone HTML, CSS and JavaScript, chosen for a portable homepage prototype that works without installation. No framework or production integration is required.

## Users
Customers browsing a merchant's personalized-product store. Merchants configure their own branding and catalog on Customelo.

## Product Purpose
Customelo is an operational ecommerce SaaS for merchants selling personalized products. The storefront supports collections, product pages, variants, personalization, cart and checkout.

## Positioning
Live Preview shows personalized text or images on a product. AI Preview generates realistic personalized-product previews. Availability depends on product configuration; the screenshots do not establish which individual products support each preview.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This project proposes only the homepage. Preserve the real application's business logic, features and merchant branding. No dashboard, deployment, checkout, backend, AI generation or production changes. Prototype navigation and catalog browsing use local presentation state only. Out-of-scope destinations are identified explicitly.

## Brand Commitments
Customelo is the platform; Demo-Store is the example merchant. The merchant's brand takes precedence in the storefront. Names, colors, logo, fonts and imagery must be replaceable.

## Product Principles
- Lead with the merchant and their products.
- Explain preview capabilities without implying universal product eligibility.
- Preserve factual catalog, review and policy content.
- Label prototype behavior and keep it independent from production.
